# 📊 Global Electronics Retailer – Excel Data Analysis Project

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end data analysis of a global electronics retailer using traditional Microsoft Excel (no Power BI, no SQL, no Python).

The objective was to clean raw business data, create analytical metrics, and build insight-driven dashboards to understand customer behavior, revenue trends, store performance, and product profitability.

The entire analysis was performed using core Excel capabilities, demonstrating strong fundamentals in business analytics and reporting.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Techniques Used
- Microsoft Excel (Traditional Approach)
- Data Cleaning & Validation
- Helper & Calculated Columns
- VLOOKUP / XLOOKUP
- INDEX–MATCH
- Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
- Slicers for interactivity
- Dashboard design & layout optimization

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation & Modeling
The raw dataset was cleaned and transformed using Excel formulas and helper columns.

### Key Derived Columns Created
- Revenue  
- Cost  
- Profit  
- Order Type (One-time vs Repeat)  
- Customer Flags (Unique vs Repeat)  
- Average Order Revenue (AOR)   
- Top Category Share (TCS)  
- Store Maturity Classification (New / Mid-Age / Matured)

Multiple datasets (Sales, Customers, Products, Stores) were joined using lookup functions (VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, INDEX–MATCH), simulating relational joins without using databases.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 📌 Customer Insights
- Repeat customers contribute approximately 61% of total orders, indicating strong customer retention.
- One-time customers account for around 39%, highlighting an opportunity for targeted retention strategies.
- Gender-wise revenue contribution is almost evenly split, suggesting no strong gender bias in purchasing behavior.

---

### 📌 Revenue & Geography Insights
- The United States is the dominant revenue contributor (~$29.9M), making it the primary growth market.
- United Kingdom and Germany follow, but with significantly lower revenue contribution.
- Revenue is concentrated in top cities and states, indicating uneven geographic performance.

---

### 📌 Time-Based Trends
- Revenue and order volumes peaked during 2019, followed by a noticeable decline in 2020–2021.
- This trend suggests potential external disruptions or demand slowdowns affecting sales and fulfillment.

---

### 📌 Store Performance Insights
- Matured stores generate the highest revenue and profit, proving operational stability over time.
- New stores contribute minimal revenue, indicating a ramp-up period before achieving profitability.
- Offline stores contribute approximately 80% of total revenue, while online stores contribute about 20%, highlighting digital growth potential.

---

### 📌 Product & Category Insights
- Computers and Home Appliances are the highest revenue-generating categories.
- Desktops lead in order volume, but high order volume does not always translate into highest revenue, indicating price vs volume trade-offs.
- Some sub-categories show high order counts but moderate revenue, highlighting margin optimization opportunities.

---

## 🎯 Business Recommendations
- Strengthen customer retention strategies focused on repeat customers.
- Expand and optimize online sales channels to reduce dependence on offline stores.
- Prioritize high-margin product categories rather than purely high-volume products.
- Diversify revenue across regions to reduce over-reliance on the US market.
- Improve onboarding and performance monitoring for new stores to accelerate profitability.
